# 07. Law & regulation — Know every rule that applies to us, and follow it in the open.

**We hold:**

- We know every law we must follow, and we keep that list where anyone can read it.
- We follow the rules in the open — anyone can check that we do, not just take our word.
- When a law is unclear, we pick the meaning we could defend out loud, in public.
- We never build anything that only survives by staying hidden.
- A regulator (the official who checks that rules are followed) gets an honest answer from us, fast.

**Do:**

- List every law, license, and rule we must follow on one page anyone can read.
- Name one person in charge of each rule on that list.
- Check the list on the same date every year and write down what changed.
- Show how we follow the rules the way we show our money: in records anyone can check.
- When a rule is unclear, write down the meaning we picked and why.
- Answer a regulator's question honestly, in plain words, within days — not months.
- When we break a rule, say so first, fix it, and publish the fix.
- Before we build anything new, ask: which rules apply to it, and can we name them?

**Don't:**

- Don't release anything if we can't explain, in one short paragraph, why it is legal.
- Don't pick a meaning of the law that we would whisper but not say on stage.
- Don't hide behind borders, shell companies (companies that exist only on paper), or silence.
- Don't make a regulator dig for an answer we already have.
- Don't treat a fine (a money penalty) as a price we can pay. A fine means we failed.

**Why:**

Rules only protect people if anyone can check that we follow them.
If we keep a public list of the laws we must follow, then anyone — a customer,
a regulator, a tired stranger — can compare our work to that list and
see for themselves. If something we build must stay hidden to survive,
it has already broken this standard, even if no court has noticed yet.
So we follow the law where everyone can watch us do it, and when we
get it wrong, we are the first to say so.

**The test:**

Can a stranger find, on one page, every rule we must follow — and read the meaning we picked for each unclear rule?
